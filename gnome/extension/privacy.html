Privacy

Quotaline for GNOME reads credentials already stored by supported coding tools. It does not ask for passwords, create an account, send telemetry, or upload crash reports. Quota requests go directly to the providers.

Local data

Settings are stored in ~/.config/quotaline/, history in ~/.local/share/quotaline/, and the last snapshot in ~/.cache/quotaline/. The service restricts these files to the current user. History retention, CSV export and clearing are controlled from Settings. Clearing history does not sign out provider clients.

The optional usage.json export contains provider IDs, plan names, times and quota rows. It excludes credentials and account identities. Hiding account details removes them from the panel state and visible cards; it does not erase cached source data. Exported CSV files remain where you save them.

Credentials and diagnostics

Credentials stay in the Rust service process and are excluded from panel state, snapshots, history and diagnostics. Token refresh is off by default. If enabled, the service may save refreshed Claude, Codex or Gemini credentials to their existing file or Secret Service item. Antigravity keeps refreshed tokens in memory; Cursor and Copilot credentials remain read-only. The Log section stores bounded service events in owner-only local files. Its view excludes entries older than seven days. You can filter and copy entries or clear the log files. Service output is also available with journalctl --user -u quotaline-gnome. Inspect logs and diagnostics before sharing them.

The menu bar app opens a SwiftUI panel with **Usage**, **History** and **Settings**. Desktop widgets mirror selected
readings without fetching provider data. Choose an [installation channel](/macos/install/), then
[connect a provider](/start/connect/).

The [shared reference](/reference/) explains quota readings, History and settings used across frontends.

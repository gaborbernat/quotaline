**Detailed logging** starts off. **Copy**, **Clear** and **Show Full Log** sit beside the bounded log viewer with level
and text filters. macOS opens a larger searchable window for the full log. GNOME expands a paged viewer in Settings.

Detailed logs include refresh timings, panel geometry, tab measurements and status-width decisions.
[Diagnostics guidance](/troubleshooting/diagnostics/) explains what to collect and what to redact.
